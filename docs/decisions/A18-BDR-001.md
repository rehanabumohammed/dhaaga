# A18-BDR-001 — Business Decision Record

## Record Identity

- Record ID: A18-BDR-001

- Decision Date: 2026-09-13

- Approving Authority: Business Owner

- Approval Identity: Business Owner — Rehan

## Business Need

Prevent selected internal commercial/accounting terminology from

unintentionally appearing in staff-facing language while allowing

Dhaaga's business vocabulary to evolve through controlled business

governance.

## Decision

The term `debit` is approved as the initial A18 V1

staff-restricted enforcement term.

## Classification

- decision_status: approved

- audience_policy: staff-restricted

## Affected Scope

English staff-facing application message values within the A18 V1

scanner scope.

This decision does not prohibit the term from internal, administrative,

technical, accounting, database, or other non-staff-facing contexts.

## Conditions

1. This is the initial V1 policy and is non-exhaustive.

2. Future vocabulary additions, amendments, deferrals, or withdrawals

   shall proceed through the Controlled Business Evolution Gate.

3. The architecture shall be reopened only if an approved business

   requirement cannot be represented by the existing policy model or

   requires a change to a locked architectural invariant.

4. This decision does not authorize accounting logic, transaction

   processing, workflow implementation, or Access Governance.

5. Technical implementation shall occur only after the appropriate

   engineering authorization gate.
